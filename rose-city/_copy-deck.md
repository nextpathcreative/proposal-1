## Merge fields — set these, then find-replace

**Per-prospect (changes each time):**
- `{{CLIENT}}` — the prospect's business name (e.g. Rivera Plumbing)
- `{{CITY}}` — their main city / metro (e.g. Sacramento)
- `{{TRADE}}` — their trade as a noun (e.g. plumbing)
- `{{TRADE_PRO}}` — their trade as a person noun (e.g. plumber)

**Your constants (set once, reuse for everyone):**
- `{{MY_BUSINESS}}` — your business name
- `{{MY_NAME}}` — your first name
- `{{MY_PHONE}}` — your phone
- `{{MY_EMAIL}}` — your email
- `{{TEST_PERIOD}}` — the free test window (e.g. 30 days)
- `{{GO_LIVE}}` — build turnaround (e.g. 5 business days)
- `{{DEMO_URL}}` — link to your live demo page (the Cornerstone build)

**Brand:** prospect's logo + colors applied throughout, per your customization step.

## 3 · Why this works

**Eyebrow:** Why this works

**Headline:** The people tapping your Google listing are ready right now.

**Body:**
When someone searches "{{TRADE}} near me" or "{{TRADE}} in {{CITY}}" and taps the website link on your Google listing, they're not browsing. They've got a problem and they're deciding who to call — often that same day.

**Stat block A:**
76% of people who search for something nearby on their phone visit a business within a day.
*Caption: Think with Google*

**Stat block B:**
About 28% of those nearby searches end in a purchase.
*Caption: Think with Google*

**Body (continued):**
That tap is the most valuable moment you get online. The only thing that matters is what happens in the few seconds after it.

Most trades websites were built to look fine on a desktop years ago. On a phone, they load slowly, bury the phone number, and make someone pinch and zoom to find anything. And people don't wait around:

**Stat block C:**
53% of visitors leave a mobile page if it takes longer than 3 seconds to load.
*Caption: Google*

**Body (close):**
When a ready-to-book customer hits a slow or cluttered page, they don't try harder — they tap back to Google and call the next {{TRADE_PRO}} on the list. You earned the click and lost the job in the same ten seconds. A landing page exists so that doesn't happen.

## 5 · How the test works

**Eyebrow:** How the test works

**Headline:** It's reversible, and it changes exactly one thing.

**Steps:**

**1. I build the page.** Your logo, your colors, your reviews, real photos. Ready in {{GO_LIVE}} from the day you say go.

**2. We point your Google link at it.** The "website" button on your Google Business Profile points to the new page for the test. That's the only change — it takes about a minute, and it can be undone just as fast.

**3. You keep your site.** Your current website stays live and untouched at its own address. I even link to it from the new page, so nothing disappears.

**4. We see what happens.** Over {{TEST_PERIOD}}, you watch whether the calls and bookings pick up. Then you decide.

**Close:** At no point are you locked in, and at no point does your existing site go away.
